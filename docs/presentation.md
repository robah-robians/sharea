# Share Hope — The Gap We Are Bridging

## The Problem

Kenya's charitable sector is large and growing, yet it is held back by a single, persistent obstacle: **a deep trust deficit between donors and NGOs.**

Donors who genuinely want to give are stopped by questions they cannot answer:

- *Is this organisation real and registered?*
- *Where does my money actually go?*
- *Will I receive any proof that my donation was received?*
- *How do I know the campaign goal is legitimate?*

On the other side, legitimate NGOs struggle to reach donors because they have no affordable, credible digital presence to prove their authenticity and showcase their work.

The result is a broken cycle — willing donors hold back, deserving causes go underfunded, and the communities that need help most are left behind.

---

## The Gap

| What Donors Experience | What NGOs Experience |
|---|---|
| No easy way to verify an NGO's legitimacy | No centralised platform to publish verified campaigns |
| No visibility into how funds are used | No tools to track donations or communicate impact |
| No formal receipt or record of giving | No affordable way to reach a wider donor base |
| Fear of fraud and misuse of funds | Difficulty building trust with the public |

This gap is not a lack of generosity — it is a lack of **infrastructure for trust.**

---

## How Share Hope Bridges It

Share Hope is built specifically to close this gap through three core pillars:

### 1. Verified Legitimacy
Every NGO on the platform must pass an Admin approval process before they can publish a single campaign. Donors never see an unverified organisation. This turns the platform itself into a trust signal.

### 2. Radical Transparency
- Interactive maps show donors the exact geographical location of every verified NGO.
- Real-time progress bars show how close a campaign is to its funding goal.
- Donors can see campaign descriptions, images, and goals before committing a single shilling.

### 3. Secure, Accountable Transactions
- M-Pesa integration means donors can give directly from their phones — the way Kenyans already transact.
- Every donation generates an instant, downloadable PDF receipt for personal tax records.
- All financial data is protected by bank-grade security (BCrypt, CSRF tokens, PDO prepared statements).

---

## The Outcome

Share Hope transforms the act of giving from a leap of faith into an **informed, secure, and transparent decision.**

Donors gain confidence. NGOs gain credibility. Communities gain funding. The gap closes.
